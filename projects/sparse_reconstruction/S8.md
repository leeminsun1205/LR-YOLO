# S8: coverage-guided object/uniform reconstruction

Implemented as sample_coverage_regions in sampling.py and sparse_case=8 in
SelectiveReconstructionDetector. S7 remains reserved. Native model/config files
unchanged. No learned tensors added; full detection supervision is preserved.

Two patches, core192/context256. Use the S6 anchor/uniform draw unchanged. Among
up to8positions (baseline, center,6jitter), maximize anchor-box area covered by
L1 core, then minimize overlap with uniform core. Baseline is included, so
anchor coverage cannot regress on identical visible GT/seed; AP may regress.
Visible boxes clipped defensively; empty images fall back to two uniform crops.
Candidate ranking uses only rectangle arithmetic; only2patches reach decoder.
Loss weights/normalization and decoder halos unchanged. Uniform may contain an
object; selected patch need not cover an entire large object.

S8 GPU audit checks actual patch-loss and Z-gradient equivalence plus S6
anchor/uniform identity and coverage non-regression. Nonempty augmented GT is
required to exercise the new branch. Audit reports are source/code/case specific.
Old S6 checkpoints keep old semantics; do not bypass resume contract checks.

For the configurable notebook see parent project notebooks/selective_reconstruction/
S8_CELLS.md. Source fixtures validate geometry but do not prove CUDA correctness,
training speed/VRAM reductions or improved AP. Main controls remain S0/S1/S3/S6.
